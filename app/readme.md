# UWI Bus Simulaition
A management simulation platform for the UWI-PTSC Service where users allocate buses to the bus route based on student requests

## Purpose of Project
Transit operations require a lot of resources, and incoveniences like buses transporting with little to no passengers, trips during low-demand times, and insufficient resouces during busy times lead to extra costs and lower service quality. 

By applying data simulation techniques we can model the full complexity of bus operations and systematically test a wide range of schedule configurations before any real-world changes are made. This approach allows decision-makers to identify schedule(s) with the highest operation efficiency minimal risk and cost 

## Features
*   scenario based simulations
*   generated schedules based on passenger frequency and student requests
*   operation efficiency reports
